## 🇨🇳 中文

把创意或已有的白模视频加上外观参考图，转成可编辑的 Blender 预演和风格化成片。先在 Blender 里完成建模与镜头预演，审核动作、空间和镜头关系，再用整组参考图做身份映射与风格化，同时处理运镜、动作连续性和声音重建。它把「几何运动载体」和「角色表演」解耦：载体只表达运动，动作语义与姿态归属单独定义，旧的人形预演也能迁移进来。适合动作短片、人物对话、车辆追逐和产品调度等有明确空间关系的场景；不用于生产级角色资产或普通剪辑。

### ✨ 能做什么

- 创意或白模视频 + 外观参考图 → 可编辑 Blender 预演
- 先审核动作、空间与镜头，通过后才进成片生成
- 整组参考图驱动身份映射与风格化，保住角色一致性
- 运镜设计与动作连续性处理，旧人形预演可迁移
- 声音重建，成片带同步音频
- 生成前做能力与声音预检，选定分辨率再出片

## 🇬🇧 English

Turns a creative brief or an existing white-model video plus appearance references into an editable Blender previs and a stylized final cut. You block out modeling and camera in Blender, review motion, space and lens choices, then map identity and style from the full reference set while handling camera moves, motion continuity and sound reconstruction. Geometry and motion ride on a carrier that expresses movement only, while action semantics and pose ownership are defined separately — so older humanoid previs can be migrated in too. Built for action shorts, dialogue scenes, car chases and product staging with clear spatial relationships; not for production-grade character assets or ordinary editing.

### ✨ What it does

- Creative brief or white-model video plus references in, editable Blender previs out
- Motion, space and lens reviewed and approved before any final render starts
- The whole reference set drives identity mapping and stylization for consistency
- Camera design and motion continuity, with migration for older humanoid previs
- Sound reconstruction so the final cut ships with synced audio
- Capability and audio prechecks plus resolution choice before generating
